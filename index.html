Our Story

Roasting small batches with care since 2019

Ember & Bean started in a garage with a second-hand roaster and two friends who were tired of stale supermarket coffee. We sold our first bags to neighbours, and word spread fast. Today we roast every order fresh, buy directly from farmers at fair prices, and deliver to homes, offices and cafés across the city.

How fresh is your coffee?

Very fresh. We roast to order, so your beans are roasted after you place your order and usually reach you within 48 hours. The roast date is printed on every bag.

Can I skip or pause a delivery?

Yes. You can skip, pause or cancel your subscription at any time, with no fees and no questions asked. Just let us know before your next roast day.

What grind options do you offer?

We offer whole bean, plus ground for espresso, pour-over, French press and filter machines. Tell us how you brew and we will grind it to match.

Do you deliver outside the city?

We deliver nationwide. Delivery times outside the city are slightly longer, but your coffee is still roasted to order and packed to stay fresh.

Can I change my plan later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade or switch between Home, Office and Café plans whenever your needs change.

Still have questions?

Our team is happy to help with anything from choosing a roast to setting up a wholesale order for your café. Send us a message and we will get back to you within a day.
